The AOK Wallet is the wallet for iOS, Android and the web browser. It is an HD wallet with BIP39 recovery words, for balances, tokens, sending and receiving, and it needs no node of its own: it reads the chain through the API.

Every way to get it is listed at [@@site_host@@/products/wallet](@@site@@/products/wallet/), each with its state. Install it only from the places named there.

> [!WARNING]
> The recovery words are the wallet. Whoever has them can spend its coins and tokens, and without them a wallet on a lost or broken phone cannot be brought back. Write them down, keep them where only you find them, and give them to nobody: no service and no developer needs them.

The desktop wallet does not create recovery words: its backup is the wallet file. See [[back-up-and-restore]].
